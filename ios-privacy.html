NoDoomScroll for iPhone and iPad — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026
Publisher: Lev Shabalin
Privacy contact: shabalinlev01@gmail.com

This policy describes the NoDoomScroll iOS app’s protected Instagram browser and its bundled Safari extension. Mac-specific privacy information is separate. iOS support and controls explain how to manage the app.

Processing inside the app

NoDoomScroll has no developer-operated account system, analytics service or backend for browsing content. It does not upload page addresses, posts, messages, login details or browsing-time totals to the developer. The blocker inspects page URLs and website structure locally to recognize known Reels feeds, hide entry points and isolate a selected video. Website credentials are not sent through a native script message bridge to the developer.

The native app reads a few numeric background-color values from the visible page to match the status area. Those values are used for appearance in memory, not sent to the developer or stored as browsing records.

Local app data

Protection preferences are saved in local app storage. The app also saves whether the introductory guide has been acknowledged and the current picture-guide step, so you can resume setup. Finishing the guide resets its step; it does not record a detected or installed personal automation.

The usage feature stores a current-day marker and aggregate today/total website-browsing durations locally. It does not store page addresses, titles, posts, messages or a detailed activity timeline in that usage record. The app measures eligible foreground browsing inside NoDoomScroll, excluding native menus, recognized sign-in routes and background time. It does not measure other apps.

The floating settings button’s corner and current-page navigation state are kept in memory while the browser runs. The app does not create a separate persistent native browsing-history database. WebKit website storage and caches, described below, are separate from these native app preferences and counters. Device backups and system data handling are controlled by iOS; the app does not operate its own cloud sync.

Instagram website data and network activity

The app uses WebKit’s default persistent website data store. Cookies, sign-in sessions, caches and website storage can remain between launches until cleared or removed by the system. Instagram’s website receives network requests and may process your IP address, device/browser information, account login, posts, messages, media interactions, advertisements and other website activity under its own policies. This policy does not say that Instagram or Meta collects no data.

Login and message content are website data; the app’s local protection does not change Instagram’s processing or make the connection anonymous. The blocker is not a VPN or network firewall and does not stop all website requests or tracking. Links opened outside NoDoomScroll are handled by the chosen app or browser under its own policies.

Safari extension permissions

The bundled Safari extension has separate settings and website permissions. If you enable it, Safari asks for permission because the extension reads page URLs and website structure and changes supported pages to hide known short-video content. Its shared rules cover supported YouTube, Instagram and Facebook website pages. This is separate from the iOS app’s Instagram-focused WKWebView browser.

Extension processing runs locally and its blocking preferences are stored locally. It does not upload browsing content to a developer server. The supported websites can still process visits and make their own requests. You can revoke website permissions or disable the extension in Safari’s settings.

Shortcuts and notifications

Optional redirection uses a personal automation that you create in Apple Shortcuts. NoDoomScroll receives a request to open protected browsing or a supported Instagram link. It does not install or inspect your personal automation list, receive the official Instagram app’s notification payload or transfer its current conversation. The guide’s saved progress is not proof of an installed automation. Apple operates Shortcuts and the system’s app-launch handling.

Your controls

Settings → Privacy & help → Clear website data removes the app’s persistent WebKit website data and discards its current page/back-forward state. This signs you out of website sessions. It retains protection choices, guide/onboarding preferences and usage totals. Settings → Usage → Reset usage totals separately clears the aggregate counters without signing you out.

You can pause protection, manage the personal automation in Shortcuts, revoke Safari extension website access, disable the extension or remove the app. There is no developer-operated NoDoomScroll account or browsing database to delete. Website account data remains subject to the website provider’s own controls.

Support, this website and Apple services

If you email support, the publisher receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond. Do not send passwords, verification codes, cookies or unredacted private conversations. Support email uses Gmail. Contact the publisher with questions about support correspondence.

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it may involve standard connection information processed by that hosting service. The app opens the support site when you choose Contact support; it does not upload your browsing content or usage totals there. Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads and distribution under its own policies.

NoDoomScroll Privacy Policy

The iPhone and iPad app has a separate privacy policy.

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Publisher: Lev Shabalin
Privacy contact: shabalinlev01@gmail.com

NoDoomScroll is a Safari extension that blocks known YouTube Shorts and Instagram/Facebook Reels website content. This policy covers the NoDoomScroll Mac app and its Safari extension.

Data stays on your device

NoDoomScroll does not collect personal data or send browsing data to the developer. It has no user accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking service, or backend server.

The extension stores four preferences locally: whether blocking is enabled, and whether each supported site is enabled. These preferences are not uploaded by NoDoomScroll or synchronized through a NoDoomScroll account. You can change them from its Safari toolbar popup.

Website access

With your permission, the extension inspects the current page address and document structure on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. It uses this information on your device to recognize dedicated Shorts/Reels pages, hide known controls and cards, and pause blocked media. It does not save a browsing history or upload page content.

Safari asks for website access because this work requires reading and changing pages. You choose which supported sites can be accessed. You can change access or disable the extension in Safari Settings, then reload open tabs.

The extension does not block the websites' own background requests or change their privacy policies. When you use these websites, the websites and your browser may process data under their own policies. Apple also processes App Store purchases and distribution under its policies; NoDoomScroll does not operate its own payment system.

If you contact support

If you choose to contact the publisher, the publisher receives the information you send, such as your email address, problem description, and any optional attachments. It is used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, verification codes, private messages, or unredacted browsing screenshots.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Correspondence is used to respond to your request, not for advertising or profiling. You can ask the publisher to delete support correspondence; copies may remain where required by law or in the email provider’s backups. The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard connection information under GitHub’s privacy policy. The app does not send data to this website automatically.

Your choices

You can pause all blocking, change individual site preferences, revoke website access, disable the extension, or remove the NoDoomScroll app. NoDoomScroll has no server-side account or browsing database to delete.

Policy changes and contact

The publisher will update this page if the app's privacy practices change. For questions, use shabalinlev01@gmail.com.

NoDoomScroll 1.0 is a Safari extension for Mac that blocks known YouTube Shorts and Instagram/Facebook Reels website content.

Get started

  1. Once NoDoomScroll is available, install it from the Mac App Store and open the app.
  2. Choose Open Safari Settings, then enable NoDoomScroll in Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  3. Allow access to the supported websites you want to block. Enable the extension in the Safari profile you use.
  4. Reload existing YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook tabs.
  5. Open NoDoomScroll in Safari's toolbar. Turn Block Shorts & Reels on and choose each site individually.

These instructions are for the signed store version. Developer settings and temporary-extension loading are not part of the normal installation.

What does it block?

The extension hides known Shorts/Reels navigation controls, shelves, and cards. Dedicated short-video URLs show a blocking screen with a link back to the site's normal home page. Media in blocked pages and identified hidden cards is paused.

Regular videos, posts, Stories, messages, and search are outside its short-video blocking rules. Videos are not classified by duration or hashtags. A regular video can still be short without being a dedicated Shorts/Reels item.

Does it work in the YouTube or Instagram app?

No. Version 1.0 covers the supported websites in Safari on Mac. It does not block native social apps or content in other browsers. Requires Safari 16 or later; requires macOS 13 or later. An iPhone/iPad release is not included in this Mac listing.

Why does Safari ask for access to website data?

The extension must inspect and modify supported pages to hide short-video content. Processing happens on your device. NoDoomScroll does not send browsing data to the developer, use analytics, or require an account.

How do I pause it?

Turn Block Shorts & Reels off in the toolbar popup. Content and navigation hidden by the extension are restored. Your individual site choices stay saved, so turning the master switch on again uses those choices. A site may need its own play control to resume media that was paused.

Nothing is being blocked

Check that NoDoomScroll is enabled for the current Safari profile, website access is allowed for the current site, the master switch is on, and the site's switch is on. Reload the tab after enabling the extension or granting access. If Safari has the toolbar button hidden, show the extension from Safari's toolbar controls.

If the popup reports a loading or saving error, choose Try again. Failed saves restore the last confirmed switch state.

I still see a short-video card or button

Websites change their layouts and labels. NoDoomScroll recognizes dedicated routes and known interface patterns; it cannot guarantee every new or unrecognized pattern is hidden immediately. A short interval can also occur during in-page navigation before a route change is detected.

Send the site, page type, Safari/macOS versions, NoDoomScroll version, and a brief description to the support contact above. An optional screenshot should be cropped and redacted to remove names, messages, account details, browsing history, and other private content. Never send login credentials or verification codes.

Does blocking stop all network requests?

No. NoDoomScroll changes the website interface and pauses identified blocked media. The website can still make background requests. It is not a network firewall or a parental-control system.

How do I remove it?

Disable NoDoomScroll in Safari → Settings → Extensions, or remove the NoDoomScroll Mac app. Reload open supported tabs afterward. To stop it on just one site, turn off that site in the popup or remove its website permission.

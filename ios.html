NoDoomScroll for iPhone and iPad — Support

NoDoomScroll opens Instagram’s website in a protected browser on iOS 17 or later. It hides known Reels feeds and discovery entry points while allowing individual shared videos when their player can be identified. This page covers the iOS app; Mac Safari instructions are separate.

Support contact: shabalinlev01@gmail.com
Privacy: NoDoomScroll iOS privacy policy

Start browsing

Open NoDoomScroll. You can follow the picture guide or tap Later to continue to Instagram’s website. Instagram may require its own sign-in for messages, private posts and some videos. The app does not require its own account. Your Instagram login belongs to the website, not a developer-operated account system.

Tap the floating settings button to find protection, the setup guide, usage, browser tools and Privacy & help. Drag the button to another corner if it covers something. Its position is kept for the current running browser, rather than saved as a permanent preference.

Optional: open here when you launch Instagram

Apple requires you to create the personal automation yourself. The official Instagram app must be installed to choose it as a trigger. NoDoomScroll cannot install or detect this automation; finishing the guide does not verify it.

  1. Open NoDoomScroll’s picture guide from Settings → Set up Instagram opening, then choose Open Shortcuts.
  2. In Shortcuts, open Automation → + / New Automation → App.
  3. Choose the official Instagram app. Select Is Opened only, leave Is Closed unselected, and choose Run Immediately.
  4. Add the NoDoomScroll → Open Instagram action. If shown, choose New Blank Automation first. Tap Done if an editor appears.
  5. Return to the Home Screen and open the official Instagram app to test. NoDoomScroll should open Instagram’s website or resume its existing page.

Guide pictures illustrate iOS 26; Apple’s labels may vary on older versions. The guide saves your place so you can return after using Shortcuts. Close/Later does not create an automation. Finish resets the guide’s saved step. To stop redirection later, disable or remove the personal automation in Shortcuts; pausing Reels protection does not disable that automation.

If an action is missing, open NoDoomScroll once and search actions again. The guide’s Action missing? help offers an alternative: add a URL action containing nodoomscroll://open/instagram, followed by Open URLs.

Can an Instagram notification open its conversation here?

The App Is Opened trigger identifies which app opened, not its notification payload or current conversation. It cannot transfer that context to NoDoomScroll. A supported Instagram HTTPS conversation or post link can be opened explicitly through Browser tools → Open a link or the Open protected link Shortcuts action, subject to the website’s login and link support.

Shared Reels and Direct messages

An individual Reel link you select can open one identified video without its next-video feed. Return to your page or conversation to choose another link. Direct message history can still scroll; the app does not turn messages into a non-scrollable page. Instagram’s own website determines which conversations and media are available to your session.

If a video needs login, is unavailable or cannot be safely separated from other players, it may remain behind a protected message. Go to Instagram home to sign in if needed, then open the selected link again. Not every video is available anonymously.

Pause or troubleshoot protection

Turn Block endless Reels off in Settings to pause protection, then turn it on to resume. A website may require its own play button to restart paused media. Protection changes the website interface using known routes and layout patterns; it is not a VPN, proxy, firewall, all-video filter or guarantee that every new layout is recognized. It does not remove all ads, posts or recommendations, or prevent Instagram’s background network requests.

If a feed entry point remains visible, check the protection switch and reload through Browser tools. For a connection error, check internet access and use Retry. Browser tools also contains Back, Forward, Open a link and Share page; a left-edge swipe can go back.

Local browsing-time totals

Settings → Usage shows today’s and total foreground website time inside NoDoomScroll. Native menus, recognized sign-in routes and background time are excluded. These figures do not measure other apps or claim time saved. Reset usage totals clears these counters without clearing your website session or protection preferences.

Sign out and clear website data

Settings → Privacy & help → Clear website data removes the app’s persistent WebKit website cookies, storage and cached data and signs you out. The app discards the current page and its back/forward state. Protection choices, guide/onboarding preferences and usage totals remain. Usage has its own reset control.

What about Safari?

The app bundles a separate Safari extension. Its Safari website permissions and settings are separate from protection inside the app’s WKWebView. Enabling the browser inside NoDoomScroll does not grant the extension Safari access. If you enable the extension in iOS Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (or Settings → Safari → Extensions on older versions), Safari asks for access to the supported websites; the shared extension has broader YouTube/Instagram/Facebook rules. The mobile app’s native browser and advertised automatic-opening workflow are Instagram-focused.

Contact support safely

Email the app/iOS version, page type and a short description. Redact names, account details, private messages and URLs from optional screenshots. Never send passwords, verification codes or session cookies. The publisher does not need your Instagram credentials to answer a support request.
